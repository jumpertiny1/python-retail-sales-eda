# Retail Sales EDA, Python & Power BI

This project analyses the Superstore dataset to understand what drives sales performance and where profitability is being generated or eroded across regions, categories, and discount levels.

## What I did
Cleaned and explored the dataset using Python, handling nulls, standardising column names, correcting data types for dates and numeric fields, and removing duplicates. Analysis covered sales and profit distribution across regions, categories, and segments, including outlier detection and discount impact analysis.

## What I found
- Furniture generated the second highest sales but the lowest profit margin
- West region led in both sales and profit contribution
- Deep discounts consistently eroded margins, with losses appearing at the higher end of the discount range
- A small number of high-value orders drove the majority of total revenue

## What it suggests
The analysis flags discount thresholds by category, particularly Furniture, as an area worth further investigation, where deeper discounts appear to correlate with margin erosion.

## Tools
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, Power BI
